# Privacy Policy for SafeMiles

**Last Updated: September 11, 2026**

## 1. Overview

SafeMiles ("we," "us," "our," or the "App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the SafeMiles mobile application.

SafeMiles is a driving practice hour logging application designed to help new drivers track their driving experience for permit requirements. We prioritize your privacy and have built the app with privacy-first principles.

## 2. What Data We Collect

### Driver Profile Information
When you create a driver profile in SafeMiles, we collect:
- Driver name
- Permit issue date
- State of residence
- Total hours required for permit completion
- Night driving hours required
- Night driving window hours (hours of the day considered "nighttime")

### Drive Records
When you log a drive, we collect:
- Start time
- End time
- Road type (Local, Highway, or Urban)
- Weather conditions (Clear, Rain, or Snow)
- Optional notes you provide

### What We Do NOT Collect

**Location Data:**
- We do NOT use CoreLocation framework
- We do NOT track GPS coordinates
- We do NOT collect address or location data
- We do NOT know where you drive, only when and how long

**Device Data:**
- We do NOT collect device identifiers
- We do NOT track app usage analytics
- We do NOT collect crash reports
- We do NOT collect any telemetry data

**Personal Data:**
- We do NOT collect your age, email, or phone number
- We do NOT create user accounts or require authentication
- We do NOT collect biometric data
- We do NOT collect health or fitness data

## 3. How We Store Your Data

### Local Storage
All data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData's local database. Your data remains on your phone and is not automatically uploaded to any server.

### iCloud Backup
SafeMiles uses Apple's CloudKit service for optional automatic cloud backup. This means:
- Your data can be automatically synced to your iCloud account (if enabled in iOS Settings)
- The backup is encrypted by Apple
- Only you can access your iCloud backup using your Apple ID
- iCloud backups persist even after uninstalling the app

**To disable iCloud sync:** Go to Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud and toggle off SafeMiles, or disable iCloud for the App in Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud > Apps & iCloud Drive.

### Data Security
- All data is stored on device-only or in your private iCloud account
- We do not store data on third-party servers
- Your data is never shared with anyone else
- We use Apple's built-in security features to protect your information

## 4. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for:
- Tracking your driving practice hours
- Calculating progress toward your permit requirements
- Generating driving statistics (total hours, night hours, etc.)
- Exporting driving records as PDF for submission to your state DMV

Your data is never used for:
- Marketing or advertising
- Selling to third parties
- Analytics or data mining
- Improving features (no usage analytics)
- Any purpose other than your personal driving log

## 5. Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data.

### Delete Individual Drives
You can swipe on any drive record to delete it immediately.

### Delete Your Entire Account & Data
- Open SafeMiles and navigate to your driver's dashboard
- Tap the settings icon (⚙️) in the top-right corner
- Scroll to "Delete User and All Data"
- Confirm the deletion

When you delete your account:
- All driver profile information is permanently deleted from your device
- All drive records are permanently deleted from your device
- All CloudKit backup records are permanently deleted from iCloud
- This action cannot be undone

### Delete via Device Settings
If you uninstall SafeMiles:
- Local data on your device is removed
- CloudKit data persists in your iCloud account until you manually remove it
- To fully remove all data from iCloud, go to Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud and toggle off SafeMiles

## 6. Third-Party Services

### Apple CloudKit
SafeMiles uses Apple CloudKit for optional cloud backup. Apple's privacy policy applies to CloudKit services: https://www.apple.com/privacy/

### No Third-Party Integrations
SafeMiles does not integrate with:
- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Social media platforms
- Data brokers
- Any third-party cloud services

## 7. Children's Privacy

SafeMiles is intended for use by teenagers and adults obtaining their driver's permit. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete such information immediately.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Your continued use of SafeMiles after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new Privacy Policy.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain privacy rights:

### Right to Access
You can access all your personal data through the SafeMiles app.

### Right to Delete
You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app settings.

### Right to Data Portability
You can export your drive records as a PDF at any time.

## 11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Delete personal information collected from you
- Opt-out of the sale of personal information

SafeMiles does not sell personal information. You can exercise your rights by deleting your account through the app settings.

## 12. GDPR Compliance

If you are in the European Union, SafeMiles complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
- Restrict processing
- Data portability

You can exercise these rights by using the delete account feature in app settings.
